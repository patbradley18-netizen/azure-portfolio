Veteran employment, pulled weekly from BLS

Five Current Population Survey series for veterans and nonveterans, 18 and over, January 2000 to September 2026. An Azure Function pulls them every Saturday with a registered BLS key held in Key Vault, and upserts into Azure SQL. Not seasonally adjusted: compare the same month across years, not adjacent months.

Unemployment rate, 2000 to September 2026

Veterans have run below nonveterans for most of the period; the Gulf War-era II series (veterans who served after September 2001) is younger and more volatile, and starts in 2006.

Labor-force participation

Veteran participation is far below nonveteran participation. Read this as age, not willingness: the veteran population skews older, and many are retired. A fair comparison would hold age constant; this series does not.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Population Survey, series LNU04049526, LNU04066408, LNU04049601, LNU01349526, LNU01349601 (monthly, not seasonally adjusted), via api.bls.gov.
